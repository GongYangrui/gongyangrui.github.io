# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

The primary audience is recruiters and hiring managers evaluating Yangrui Gong for backend engineering and AI application roles. Secondary audiences include engineers, potential collaborators, and people who discover the site through a technical article.

Visitors should be able to understand who Yangrui is, what he is interested in, how he approaches technical work, and how to contact him without reading a conventional academic CV from top to bottom.

## Product Purpose

This site is Yangrui Gong's public professional home. It should present a clear, current professional narrative around backend engineering and practical AI applications, while preserving earlier research experience as supporting evidence rather than the main identity.

The site succeeds when a recruiter can quickly form a credible impression, find relevant experience and writing, and continue to GitHub, a project, or direct contact. Technical writing should demonstrate curiosity and sustained engagement even before the project portfolio is extensive.

The primary language is English. Chinese is a complete secondary language that visitors can switch to explicitly.

## Brand Personality

Clear, technically curious, and grounded. The voice should feel thoughtful and direct rather than self-promotional. Technology should be communicated through precision, structure, and small details, not visual spectacle.

## Anti-references

- Academic homepage templates that read like a chronological CV or publication list.
- Generic developer portfolios built from repeated icon cards and skill-percentage meters.
- Cyberpunk neon, particle backgrounds, glowing terminals, excessive grids, and other literal "technology" decoration.
- Heavy glassmorphism, oversized gradients, scroll-jacking, long entrance animations, or effects that compete with the content.
- Dense dashboards or overly clever navigation that makes a recruiter learn how the site works.
- Empty claims such as "passionate developer" without writing, decisions, or concrete evidence.

## Design Principles

1. **Make the current direction unmistakable.** Backend engineering and AI applications lead the story; academic work becomes concise supporting context.
2. **Evidence before claims.** Use articles, notes, project decisions, and concrete experience to demonstrate technical curiosity.
3. **One clear path per visit.** A recruiter should always know whether to read work, browse writing, open a project, or make contact.
4. **Content is data, not layout.** Resume facts, projects, links, and bilingual text live in structured content and automatically populate every relevant page.
5. **Quiet technology.** Create a technical feeling through typography, information hierarchy, syntax-aware details, and restrained motion rather than decoration.
6. **Publishing must stay lightweight.** Routine profile, project, and blog updates should not require editing components or deployment configuration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for contrast, focus states, semantics, keyboard navigation, and readable text sizing. Respect reduced-motion preferences and avoid interactions that rely only on color, hover, or animation. Support responsive layouts from small phones through wide desktop screens. Keep both language versions structurally equivalent and declare language changes correctly for assistive technology.
